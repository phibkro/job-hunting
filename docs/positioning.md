# Positioning (Phase 0)

Hard constraints and profile facts that steer targeting and CV framing.
Update as decisions land; this is the single source of truth for "what
band are we applying in".

## Established

- **Band: entry-level / junior.** No prior technical employment — theoretical
  expertise exists but no work artifacts or formal qualifications to prove
  seniority. Do not target roles requiring provable senior experience
  (e.g. senioringeniør titles → out of range; killed P-158 on this).
- **Education: Informatics bachelor at NTNU, incomplete.** 3 years done,
  2 failed exams remain before the degree is awarded.
  - CV framing (Philip, 2026-08-25): write "Informatikk(studier) — NTNU"
    with dates only. Never claim the degree, but do NOT volunteer exam
    status on the CV either — Philip discloses the outstanding exams
    verbally if asked. Transcript uploads (public sector) still govern:
    the CV must never contradict the karakterutskrift.
  - Listings requiring "fullført bachelorgrad" as minimum krav are hard
    gates in the public sector (kvalifikasjonsprinsippet) → out of range
    until the degree is done.
  - Listings phrased "relevant høyere utdanning; erfaring kan kompensere"
    are in range — 3 years informatics is relevant høyere utdanning.
  - **Strategy: finishing the 2 exams unlocks the bachelor-gated band.**
    Roles like P-156 (min. bachelorgrad, 540–667k) become reachable.
- **Role thesis (confirmed 2026-07-09):** software engineering is the
  hobby/passion track, not the career track. The job should be **bounded**
  (work stays at work: no on-call, no take-home problems) and spend the
  *social* energy channel, preserving the deep-focus budget for passion
  projects. Ikigai frame: exercise technical expertise + feel connected
  + useful to wider society. Support-band roles are the target *by
  preference*, not as a fallback — this dissolves the "overqualified
  flight risk" objection in applications, and can be said honestly.
  Corollary: pure dev roles compete with passion projects for the same
  cognitive budget → deprioritized (P-160 Spond = lottery ticket only).
- **Location:** Oslo.
- Open to adjacent dev roles at near-zero application cost
  (e.g. Spond "søk uten CV" — P-160), but support band is the target.
